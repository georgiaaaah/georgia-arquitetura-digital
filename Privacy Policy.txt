

Privacy Policy

At geōrgia, transparency is the foundation of our technology. This policy describes how we handle the data collected on this website to ensure a secure and intentional digital presence.

1. Data Collection and Purpose
We process only the data strictly necessary for the execution of our services:

Direct Communication: When using the WhatsApp links to start a project, the data provided (such as name and phone number) is used exclusively for initial customer service, support, and the preparation of business proposals.

No Unsolicited Marketing: We do not use your contact information for broadcast lists, newsletters, or any other marketing purposes without your explicit and separate consent.

2. Legal Basis for Processing
The processing of your data is based on your consent when voluntarily initiating a conversation through our channels, and on pre-contractual procedures, aiming to fulfill your requests for quotes and consultancy.

3. Use of Interface Technologies (Cookies and LocalStorage)
To ensure your browsing experience is personalized and functional, we use local storage (localStorage) to:

Save your language preference (pt or en).

Remember your choice of interface theme (Dark or Light).
These are strictly functional technologies and do not track your behavior on other websites.

4. User Rights (Access and Deletion)
In compliance with data protection laws (GDPR/LGPD), you have the right to:

Request confirmation of the existence of data processing.

Request the permanent deletion of your contact data from our records at any time. To do so, simply send a message via our WhatsApp with the phrase "Remove Data."

5. Security and Sharing
Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Conversations via WhatsApp are protected by end-to-end encryption by the Meta platform. Internally, we adopt technical measures to ensure that only the personnel necessary for your project have access to the information shared.
